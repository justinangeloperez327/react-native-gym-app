## Proposed store description

Log your training on your own terms with a local-first fitness journal. Plan reusable workout routines, record strength and cardio sessions, review training history and personal records, and track body measurements and goals. Add your own food nutrition values to log meals, daily macros and water. Record optional sleep and recovery check-ins without automatically prescribed targets.

Features:
- Offline workout tracking and scheduled routines.
- Local training history, exercise progression and personal records.
- Body measurements, weight goals and daily nutrition/water logging.
- Personal food library with archived-food history preservation.
- Optional daily sleep and wellbeing journal.
- JSON export and confirmed restore, controlled by the user.

**Important:** This app is a journal, not a healthcare or diagnostic tool. Nutrition values are manually entered and may be inaccurate. No medical advice or prescribed goals are provided. Backups are unencrypted and must be stored carefully.
